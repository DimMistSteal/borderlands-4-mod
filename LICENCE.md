## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Combat Mods
- Aimbot — auto-aim with customizable FOV and smoothing
- Silent Aim — hit targets without visible snap
- Damage Multiplier — increase outgoing damage (1x–100x)
- Infinite Ammo — no reload and unlimited magazines
- No Recoil — remove weapon kick

### 🗺️ Visuals & ESP
- Loot ESP — highlight chests, weapons, and gear through walls
- Enemy ESP — see enemies with health bars and distance
- Item Rarity Filter — show only legendary and epic loot
- Teleport — instantly move to waypoints or loot
- Speedhack — adjust game speed (0.1x to 5x)

- God Mode — become invincible
- Infinite Money — set your cash to any amount
- Skill Point Editor — unlock all skill trees
- Instant Level Up — reach max level instantly
- No Clip — fly through terrain
